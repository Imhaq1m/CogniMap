  never moves on by itself; the user always reviews the feedback first.

  it opens the "Level 3 Complete!" banner (Level 4 handoff); after a mid-run

  plain close.

  the uncleared scenario via store.active_scenario, manual Generate reopens it.

  resubmit after a clear can never force-advance on stale state.
